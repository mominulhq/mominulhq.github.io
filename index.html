About

I work on deep learning and computer vision, with particular depth in medical image analysis — retinal vessel segmentation, echocardiographic segmentation, and diagnostic classification — and a growing line of work in low-light surveillance imaging and geospatial land-cover analysis. My approach centers on lightweight, explainable architectures: hybrid CNN-Transformer designs and adaptive filtering frameworks built to stay accurate under real-world distribution shift, whether that shift comes from a different imaging device, degraded surveillance footage, or a different geographic region. I completed my MSc (Research) in Information Technology at Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS, where my thesis introduced MVT-Net, a lightweight hybrid CNN-Transformer for retinal vessel segmentation with built-in explainability. I have authored seven papers across IEEE, Elsevier, and Wiley venues and currently review for Biomedical Signal Processing and Control (Elsevier, Q1). I am looking for a PhD, Research Scientist, or Research Assistant position where I can extend this work further into applied computer vision — image classification, object detection, and monitoring systems for healthcare, security, and agriculture.
