About

I completed my MSc (Research) in Information Technology at Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS, where my thesis introduced MVT-Net, a lightweight hybrid CNN-Transformer for retinal vessel segmentation with built-in explainability. My work sits at the intersection of computer vision, medical image analysis, and explainable AI, with a focus on models that are accurate enough for clinical use and interpretable enough to be trusted in one. I have authored seven papers across IEEE, Elsevier, and Wiley venues and currently review for Biomedical Signal Processing and Control (Elsevier, Q1). I am looking for a PhD, Research Scientist, or Research Assistant position where I can extend this line of work on efficient, explainable vision models for healthcare.
